🍅 Tomato Leaf Spot

Symptoms: Brown spots on leaves.

Treatment: Remove infected leaves and use fungicide.

🥔 Potato Late Blight

Symptoms: Dark patches on leaves.

Treatment: Use fungicide and disease-resistant varieties.

🌾 Rice Blast

Symptoms: Diamond-shaped spots on leaves.

Treatment: Proper drainage and fungicide spray.

🧵 Cotton Wilt

Symptoms: Yellow leaves and wilting.

Treatment: Healthy seeds and crop rotation.

🌿 Medicinal Plant Diseases

Tulsi Leaf Spot, Aloe Vera Rot, Neem Leaf Blight.

Treatment: Remove affected parts and maintain plant hygiene.
